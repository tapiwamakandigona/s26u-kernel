S688LN kernel kit v0.6 - "signed modules" (exact-stamp kernel + WiFi fix)
==========================================================================

WHY v0.6: the v0.5 flash proved the version-string theory right - the
phone finally FOUND the missing WiFi/BT modules. But it refused to load
Google's rfkill module because every kernel build only trusts helper
modules signed by ITS OWN build key. v0.6 ships the SAME exact-stamp
kernel PLUS its own build-matched, correctly-signed rfkill module,
auto-loaded at every boot by a tiny Magisk module.

The Magisk module is safe by design:
  - On the STOCK kernel it does NOTHING (it sees rfkill already loaded
    and exits immediately).
  - It only loads drivers, exactly like the phone does at every boot.
  - Remove it any time in the Magisk app under Modules.

HOW TO USE - one step at a time, wait for each to finish:

  1. STEP-1-CHECK.bat   - read-only. Makes/checks your stock backup.
                          (If you still have the v0.5 folder you can
                          instead copy its backup\ folder into this one.)
  2. STEP-2-FLASH.bat   - installs the WiFi-fix module into Magisk,
                          then flashes the v0.6 kernel and reboots.
  3. After boot: try the WiFi and Bluetooth switches.
  4. STEP-3-TEST.bat    - packs the result logs for Viktor, PASS or FAIL.
     Send S688LN-v06-logs.zip to Viktor on Slack.

UNDO - always available:
  RESTORE-STOCK-v2.bat  - puts your stock kernel back in 2 minutes.
                          You have already used it successfully.
  The Magisk module does nothing on stock, but you can also remove it
  in the Magisk app under Modules.

Your data, apps, Magisk root and banking apps are not touched by any
of these steps. Only the boot partition (slot a) is written, and your
verified stock backup restores it exactly.
