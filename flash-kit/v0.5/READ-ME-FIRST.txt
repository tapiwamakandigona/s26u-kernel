KERNEL v0.5 "EXACT STAMP" - READ ME FIRST
==========================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
Both kernels we tried before carried the WRONG internal version
stamp: "maybe-dirty" instead of your stock kernel's exact stamp.
Your phone stores 14 WiFi/Bluetooth driver files in a folder
NAMED AFTER that exact version stamp - so the old builds could
never find them. Everything else loaded fine, which is why the
phone booted perfectly but WiFi and Bluetooth stayed dead.

v0.5 is the same trusted stock-source build as before, with ONE
change: the version stamp is now byte-identical to stock
(the build robot refuses to ship it otherwise).

Nothing here touches init_boot, vendor_boot, thermal, or your
data/banking. Root (Magisk) is preserved. Your verified restore
(RESTORE-STOCK-v2.bat) is included and unchanged.

HOW TO USE - one step at a time, reboot between steps
-----------------------------------------------------
0. Unzip this WHOLE zip anywhere. Everything is inside,
   including adb/fastboot. If you still have the old kit's
   backup folder you can copy backup\boot_stock_a.img over,
   but STEP 1 can also make a fresh backup by itself.

1. Double-click  STEP-1-CHECK.bat   (read-only, safe)
   Send  S688LN-check-v05.zip  to Viktor and WAIT for the GO.

2. After Viktor's GO: double-click  STEP-2-FLASH.bat
   Same routine you have done twice already.

3. After the reboot: double-click  STEP-3-TEST.bat
   It checks WiFi/BT, tries an automatic rescue if needed, and
   packs  S688LN-v05-logs.zip  for Viktor. Send it PASS or FAIL.

IF ANYTHING IS WRONG AT ANY POINT
---------------------------------
Double-click  RESTORE-STOCK-v2.bat  - the exact restore you
already used successfully today. Stock back in 2 minutes.

IF THE PHONE WILL NOT BOOT
--------------------------
1. Hold POWER about 15 seconds to force it off.
2. Hold POWER + VOLUME UP until the Recovery menu shows.
3. Volume keys to "Enter fastboot", press Power.
4. Plug in USB, double-click  RESTORE-STOCK-v2.bat
